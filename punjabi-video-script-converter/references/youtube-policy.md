## 1. SAFETY & HARMFUL CONTENT

### 1.1 Violence
- ❌ Graphic depictions of violence, gore, or death
- ❌ Content that promotes, glorifies, or incites violence against people or animals
- ❌ Threats directed at individuals or groups
- ✅ Violence in a clear educational, documentary, or historical context is permitted if not gratuitous

### 1.2 Dangerous Activities
- ❌ Instructions for activities that could cause serious injury (e.g., dangerous stunts, chemical experiments)
- ❌ Encouraging viewers to replicate dangerous behavior
- ✅ Safety demonstrations and cautionary content are permitted with appropriate warnings

### 1.3 Self-Harm & Suicide
- ❌ Content that promotes, glorifies, or provides instructions for self-harm or suicide
- ❌ Detailed methods, means, or locations associated with self-harm
- ✅ Educational content about mental health awareness, with appropriate framing and crisis resources, is permitted

### 1.4 Child Safety
- ❌ Any content that sexualizes minors
- ❌ Content that could be used to groom, exploit, or abuse children
- ❌ Content that encourages children to engage in dangerous or inappropriate behavior
- ✅ Age-appropriate educational content for children is encouraged

---

## 2. HATE SPEECH & DISCRIMINATION

- ❌ Content that promotes hatred based on: race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or immigration status
- ❌ Dehumanizing language targeting any group
- ❌ Using slurs to attack a person or group
- ✅ Educational discussions of discrimination, historical context, or counter-speech are permitted
- ✅ Comedy that does not reinforce harmful stereotypes as facts is generally permitted — use judgment

**Special note for Punjabi learning videos:** Avoid any content that could be read as disparaging
any religious community (Sikh, Hindu, Muslim, Christian), caste group, or regional identity.
Punjab has complex communal history — be neutral and inclusive in all examples and illustrations.

---

## 3. MISINFORMATION

### 3.1 Medical & Health Claims
- ❌ False health claims presented as fact (e.g., "This cures cancer")
- ❌ Discouraging viewers from seeking professional medical help
- ❌ Promoting unproven treatments as established medicine
- ✅ General wellness information with appropriate disclaimers is permitted
- ✅ Presenting medical information with "consult a doctor" framing is recommended

### 3.2 Educational Accuracy
- ❌ Presenting factually incorrect information as established truth
- ❌ Manipulating historical records in a misleading way
- ✅ Clearly labeled opinions, interpretations, and perspectives are fine
- ✅ Saying "scientists believe..." or "one view is..." frames claims appropriately

---

## 4. SENSITIVE TOPICS

### 4.1 Religion
- Present all religious references respectfully and neutrally
- Do not mock, demean, or caricature any religious practice or belief
- When using religious examples in learning content, ensure accuracy and respect

### 4.2 Politics
- Avoid partisan political messaging in educational content
- Do not make false claims about elections, government, or political figures
- Avoid content that could be perceived as political propaganda

### 4.3 Financial Advice
- ❌ Specific investment recommendations presented as guaranteed
- ✅ General financial literacy education is encouraged
- Add disclaimers when discussing financial topics: frame as education, not advice

---

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & COPYRIGHT

- ❌ Reproducing song lyrics (even a few lines) without license
- ❌ Using copyrighted music as background without a license or YouTube Audio Library source
- ❌ Including dialogue, clips, or characters from copyrighted films/shows/games
- ❌ Reading from copyrighted books or articles verbatim
- ✅ Original scripts, original narration, and royalty-free music are safe
- ✅ Brief quotation for commentary/criticism (fair use) — keep minimal and clearly attributed

**Script check:** Confirm no branded jingles, movie quotes, or song lyrics appear in the voiceover.

---

## 6. SPAM & DECEPTIVE PRACTICES

### 6.1 Engagement Manipulation
- ❌ "Sub4sub" schemes or paid engagement solicitation
- ❌ Misleading thumbnails or titles that don't match content
- ❌ Artificially inflating engagement metrics
- ✅ Genuine, honest CTAs asking for likes, comments, and subscriptions are permitted

### 6.2 Impersonation
- ❌ Impersonating another creator, brand, or public figure
- ❌ Using another channel's name or branding deceptively

---

## 7. ADVERTISER-FRIENDLY CONTENT (for monetization)

If the channel intends to monetize, additionally avoid:
- Profanity in the title, thumbnail, or first 30 seconds
- Excessive profanity throughout (occasional mild language may be demonetized, not removed)
- Controversial or sensitive topics without strong educational framing
- Shocking or sensational content not justified by educational value

**Recommended for Punjabi learning videos:** Keep language clean, tone positive and encouraging,
and framing clearly educational. This maximizes ad eligibility.

---

## 8. CAPTION & SUBTITLE POLICIES

- Captions must accurately reflect spoken content — do not use captions to insert content not in the audio
- Do not use captions to add hidden keywords for SEO manipulation
- Auto-generated captions may differ — manual captions (provided in the script) are preferred
- Captions must comply with all content policies (no hate speech, harassment, etc.)
